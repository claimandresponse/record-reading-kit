# Why an Apple decision has two dates

[Watch the captioned guide](https://claimandresponse.com/videos/#commission-decision-dates) · [English captions](captions/commission-decision-dates.vtt)

## Transcript

In the Apple state-aid case, the European Commission decision is dated 30 August 2016.

The Official Journal that carries it is dated 19 July 2017.

A useful source card records the decision date and can list the publication date separately.

## Try it

Open the Official Journal copy and look for the date in the decision heading and the date printed in the journal header. Record them separately as the decision date and the publication date.
