# What makes a reply easy to verify?

[Watch the captioned guide](https://claimandresponse.com/videos/#reply-with-evidence) · [English captions](captions/reply-with-evidence.vtt)

## Transcript

Name the page and the exact statement you are addressing.

Give your response and include the record you rely on, with a date or page number.

The editor can check the evidence and attribute your response to you.

## Try it

Draft two sentences in reply to a specific published claim. Name the passage you are answering and link the public record that supports your reply. Use the private contact channel for contact information.
