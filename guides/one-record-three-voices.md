# Three voices in a public record

[Watch the captioned guide](https://claimandresponse.com/videos/#one-record-three-voices) · [English captions](captions/one-record-three-voices.vtt)

## Transcript

Start by identifying who made the claim. Write their name beside the date.

Give the reply its own line and identify who issued it.

On the third line, write what the available records establish. If they leave a question open, keep that question visible.

## Try it

Choose an example from the archive methodology. Make three rows for the claim, reply and finding. In each row, enter the speaker or issuer, the date and the supporting record. Mark any row for which you still need a source.
