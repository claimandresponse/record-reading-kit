# When two companies share a name

[Watch the captioned guide](https://claimandresponse.com/videos/#matching-company-names) · [English captions](captions/matching-company-names.vtt)

## Transcript

Start with the full legal name in the source document.

Compare the jurisdiction and the registration number where one is available.

Check the date before treating a filing as evidence of today’s directors or ownership.

## Try it

Choose two similarly named companies and open their registry records. Compare the full registered names, jurisdictions and registration numbers. Note the date of each record before describing any relationship.
