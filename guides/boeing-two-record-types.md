[Watch the captioned guide](https://claimandresponse.com/videos/#boeing-two-record-types) · [English captions](captions/boeing-two-record-types.vtt)

## Transcript

The January 2021 document is a deferred prosecution agreement. Read paragraph two for the company’s acknowledgment of responsibility.

In November 2025, a separate district court order granted the government’s motion to dismiss the charge.

The agreement and the order document different steps. Put them on separate lines in the timeline.

## Try it

Read paragraph 2 of the agreement and the opening of the later order. For each document, record the issuer or parties, date and action described. Link each entry to its own document.
