# Build a source card in three steps

[Watch the captioned guide](https://claimandresponse.com/videos/#source-card) · [English captions](captions/source-card.vtt)

## Transcript

Copy the name of the body that issued the document.

Add its date and a reference a reader can search for, such as a docket or decision number.

Give the reader a link and the page or paragraph where the relevant passage begins.

## Try it

Choose an entry from the document index. Record its title, issuer, document type and date. Link to the original and add the page or paragraph another reader would need.
